Kaohsiung just turned chilly—time for a sherry cask.

Under BBR, Glenrothes highlighted the vintage year. Edrington bought the brand back in May 2017, and the core range switched to age statements in 2018.

Distilled in 2000 (six months on, six off) and bottled in 2017. This 58.7% cask moves from raisins and berry gummies to medium-dark roast coffee. Come unwind at Nova.

Tasting notes

Nose
Bright raisins, berry gummies, dark berries, citrus and dried fruits. Followed by caramel, nuts and vanilla. Later, oak, liquorice, cinnamon and pepper.
Palate
Medium mouthfeel. Rich raisins, berries, citrus, caramel, dark chocolate, black coffee, nuts, toasted oak, cardamom and pepper.
Finish
Medium-long. Raisins, dried fruits, citrus, dark chocolate, a hint of caramel, walnuts, oak, leather, cardamom and pepper.

You can drink this one at Nova Whisky Bar in Lingya District, Kaohsiung. Stock is limited — ask at the bar.
