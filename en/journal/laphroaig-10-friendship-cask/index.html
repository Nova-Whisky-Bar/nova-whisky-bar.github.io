A cool evening calls for something smoky.

THE FRIENDSHIP CASK is a 10-year-old Laphroaig single cask celebrating the 10th anniversary of The Drunken Master Whisky Bar in Taiwan and the 6th anniversary of Café Swing in Korea.

Café Swing's owner called it a truly delicious Laphroaig, praising its bright, layered tropical fruit aromas. The label was hand-drawn by Elaine, manager of The Drunken Master Whisky Bar, capturing both bars and the memories made in them.

Now pouring by the glass at Nova Whisky Bar. Come raise a glass to their friendship with us!

Tasting notes

2026/11/11 20:05

Pale amber
Nose
Soft peat smoke, sea salt, citrus, pineapple and tropical fruit, with a touch of iodine. Then charred notes, dark chocolate, walnut, vanilla and herbs, followed by mineral notes, ginger, mint and spice.
Palate
Medium-bodied. Salt, peat smoke, citrus, grilled pineapple, ripe fruit, malt, dark chocolate, ginger and pepper.
Finish
Medium. Clean, with sweet, fruity peat smoke, citrus, lemon, light salinity, malt, nuts, ginger, coconut, minerals and pepper.

You can drink this one at Nova Whisky Bar in Lingya District, Kaohsiung. Stock is limited — ask at the bar.
