Nova Whisky Bar新星威士忌酒館

Nova Whisky Bar (新星威士忌酒館) is a quiet whisky bar in Lingya District, Kaohsiung, Taiwan — a good place to drink on your own. The back bar is built around Scotch single malts, independent bottlings and single casks, and any dram over NT$500 can be ordered as a half pour. We are part of The Drunken Master (醉俠) family and share a team with NOVA Distillery (南曜蒸餾所), a craft distillery being built in Kaohsiung.

About

About Nova

「Just the right amount of quiet, one dram at a time.」

Nova opened as Highlander Inn Takao (高雄高地人小酒館) and became Nova Whisky Bar in April 2026. The name changed; the job didn't. We tell you where the whisky in your glass came from — the distillery, the cask, the vintage — so it makes sense as well as tastes good.

22.6°N is the latitude of Kaohsiung.

We keep the room quiet on purpose. Come alone after work and unwind, or bring a friend or two for a low-voiced chat. If you want to talk whisky, we're happy to; if you'd rather be left alone, you will be.

On social media we keep a running tasting journal in four languages, 22.6°N | Nova in the Glass — from old Gordon & MacPhail Connoisseurs Choice vintages to SMWS and other independent single casks.

How to drink

How to drink at Nova

Full and half pours

Whisky is served by the glass. Any dram priced over NT$500 is also available as a half pour — an easy way to try older or rarer single casks.

Pick by taste

Tell us what you like — sherry cask, bourbon cask, peat — and we'll line up a few drams side by side.

Independent single casks

Single casks from bottlers such as Gordon & MacPhail, Douglas Laing and SMWS, many of them a couple of hundred bottles from one cask.

A quiet room

No loud music, no rush. Comfortable on your own, just right for two or three.

Not drinking neat?

Highballs, cocktails and non-alcoholic drinks are on offer, so everyone at the table is covered.

New to whisky?

No jargon needed. Tell us what you usually drink and we'll start somewhere easy.

Tastings

Brand tastings

We regularly host small tastings with importers and brand ambassadors. Dates and how to sign up are posted on our Instagram, Facebook and Threads.

FAQ

FAQ

01What kind of bar is Nova Whisky Bar?

A quiet whisky bar in Lingya District, Kaohsiung, centred on Scotch — especially single malts, independent bottlings and single casks. It's comfortable for solo drinkers; we recommend by taste and cask style and can line up a few drams for comparison.

02I rarely drink whisky neat. Is Nova OK for beginners?

Yes. Tell us what you usually drink and whether you like it sweeter or smokier, and we'll start with something approachable. Drams over NT$500 come as half pours too, so trying several won't break the bank.

03Is it awkward to come alone? Is it quiet?

Plenty of guests come on their own. We keep the room quiet — good for unwinding by yourself or a low-voiced chat with a friend or two. Talk whisky with us if you like, or keep to yourself.

04Can I order a half pour?

Yes. Any dram priced over NT$500 is available as a half pour — handy for trying older or rarer single casks.

05My friends don't drink whisky neat. Anything for them?

Yes — Highballs, cocktails and non-alcoholic drinks.

06What whiskies do you have?

Mainly Scotch: single malts from across the regions, plus single casks from independent bottlers such as Gordon & MacPhail, Douglas Laing and SMWS. There are more than 500 bottles on the back bar.

07Where is Nova?

2F., No. 14, Ln. 128, Linsen 2nd Rd., Lingya Dist., Kaohsiung City.

08What are the opening hours?

Sunday to Friday 20:00–01:30, Saturday 20:00–02:00. Closures are announced on our social media.

09How much does it cost? Is there a minimum charge?

Minimum spend NT$500 per person, plus a 10% service charge.

10Do I need a reservation?

For larger groups or weekends, call +886 7 330 8050 or send a DM to @novawhisky on Instagram.

11Can I order in English, Japanese or Korean?

Yes, we can serve you in English. Japanese and Korean aren't available at the moment.

12Do you take cards?

Yes. We accept cash and credit cards.

13Do you host tastings?

Yes. We hold small tastings with importers and brand ambassadors from time to time. Dates and sign-up details go up on our Instagram, Facebook and Threads.

14Is Nova the same bar as Highlander Inn Takao?

Yes. Highlander Inn Takao (高雄高地人小酒館) was renamed Nova Whisky Bar (新星威士忌酒館) in April 2026.

15How is Nova related to The Drunken Master?

Nova is part of The Drunken Master (醉俠) family and shares its team with NOVA Distillery (南曜蒸餾所), a craft distillery being built in Kaohsiung.
