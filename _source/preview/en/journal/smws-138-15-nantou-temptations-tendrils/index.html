Did you make it to the polls today?

With the election behind us, regardless of the outcome, we all share the hope that the place we call home will continue to grow and thrive.

Yesterday, we introduced a whisky from Maoweiki Distillery, part of Taiwan's new generation of whisky makers. Today, let's turn back the clock and meet one of the pioneers of Taiwanese whisky: Nantou Distillery.

Founded in 1978, Nantou began by producing fruit wines to help local farmers make use of surplus fruit. After suffering devastating fire damage during Taiwan's 1999 earthquake, the distillery rebuilt and opened a new chapter in 2008 by embarking on single malt whisky production.

This bottling was selected by the Scotch Malt Whisky Society (SMWS), with the number 138 on the label identifying Nantou Distillery.

Named “Temptation's Tendrils,” its flavours seem to intertwine and unfold like delicate tendrils, inviting you to explore them layer by layer.

Bright, sweet citrus, dried pineapple and floral aromas mingle with creamy chocolate, nuts and vanilla. The palate is smooth, with plum, honey and coconut sweetness unfolding beautifully, followed by sweet-and-salty preserved fruit, gentle herbal bitterness, woody notes and pepper.

It beautifully reflects the SMWS flavour profile represented by its light blue label: Juicy, Oak & Vanilla.

From a new generation of distilleries to the pioneers who came before them, join us at Nova tonight and discover a whisky born on this very island.

Tasting notes

Nose
Bright, sweet citrus notes, dried pineapple and hints of melon, followed by florals, malt, creamy chocolate, walnuts, vanilla and herbs. Later, woody notes, cardamom, tobacco, herbs and spices.
Palate
Smooth mouthfeel. Sweet citrus and plum notes, followed by dried fruits, honey, florals, creamy chocolate, nuts, almonds, vanilla, herbs, coconut, wood and pepper.
Finish
Medium. Citrus, plum, a hint of sweet-and-salty preserved fruit, florals, malt, chocolate, herbal bitterness, woody and waxy notes, spices and pepper.
